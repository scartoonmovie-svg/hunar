🚗 Getting Here & Parking

🚗 By Car

From City Center: Take NH-19, then turn at Koirajpur Junction. School is 2km ahead on left.

Parking: Ample parking available. Follow volunteer directions for designated spots.

Free Parking: No parking fees!

🚌 By Public Transport

Bus Routes: City buses 15, 23, 45 stop at Koirajpur

Auto/Rickshaw: Easily available from all parts of Varanasi

Nearest Stop: Koirajpur (5 min walk)

🏍️ Two-Wheeler Parking

Dedicated Area: Separate two-wheeler parking zone

Security: Guarded parking with volunteers

Capacity: 200+ bikes/scooters

♿ Accessibility

Wheelchair Access: Ramps available at all entrances

Special Parking: Reserved spots near entrance

Assistance: Volunteers ready to help
