System Protocols

To ensure a secure and stabilized network for everyone, all users must strictly adhere to these guidelines

⏰ Timings & Setup

All stalls must be set up and ready by 9:00 AM for the event. Stalls must remain operational until 2:00 PM. Early closing is not permitted without prior approval from coordinators.

🧹 Data Hygiene (Cleanliness)

You are responsible for the cleanliness of your server area. A trash bin must be present at every node. A penalty of ₹500 will be imposed for data corruption (littering or leaving the area unclean).

⚡ Power Draw (Electricity Usage)

Only one 5A plug point is provided per node. High-draw appliances like microwaves are strictly prohibited. Only basic LED lighting and low-power cooktops (induction only) are allowed.

🍔 Organic Matter Safety (Food Safety)

All food nodes must maintain strict protocol. Hairnets and gloves are mandatory. Open flame cooking is strictly prohibited – only induction arrays are allowed for safety reasons.

🤝 Network Conduct (Behavior)

Maintain decorum within the grid. Aggressive spam marketing, loud frequencies, or disrupting neighboring nodes will lead to immediate warnings and possible banishment from the event.

🚫 Prohibited Assets (Banned Items)

Distribution of weapons, hazardous elements, adult content, or anything violating the system firewall is strictly forbidden. This includes any items that may harm others or violate school policies.

💰 Registration & Payments

Stall Charge: ₹200 per stall. Payment must be completed by August 4, 2026 to the teacher in-charge (Sneha Singh Ma'am). All profits from sales belong to the stall owners. The school does not take any share of the earnings.

👥 Team Regulations

Each stall can have a team of up to 4 students from grades 9-12. Team members must be registered during the initial registration process. Last-minute additions may not be permitted.

⚠️ ZERO TOLERANCE DIRECTIVE - PRIORITY RED

The system administrators (coordinators and teachers) reserve the right to immediately sever connection to any node and expel users found violating these protocols. No refunds will be provided for terminated connections.

Breaking rules may also result in disciplinary action from the school administration.

Questions About Rules?

If you need clarification on any rule or guideline, feel free to contact our coordinators.
