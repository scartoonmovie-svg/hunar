Knowledge Base

Query the system. Retrieve answers.

1. Who can register a stall?

Answer: Only students of Classes IX to XII of Sant Atulanand Convent School are eligible to register a stall for Hunar Bazaar 2026.

2. What is the stall registration fee?

Answer: The registration fee is ₹200 per stall. The fee must be submitted to your respective teacher in-charge on or before 4 August 2026.

3. Do I get to keep the profit from my stall?

Answer: Yes. The profit earned from your stall belongs entirely to the participating student(s). You are free to keep all the earnings generated during the event.

4. Who can participate in Hunar Bazaar?

Answer: Only students of Classes IX to XII who complete the registration process before the deadline are eligible to participate.

5. What items are NOT allowed?

Answer: The following items are strictly prohibited:

  • Dangerous or poisonous substances
  • Fireworks, explosives, or flammable materials
  • Weapons or sharp hazardous objects
  • Tobacco, alcohol, vaping products, or any illegal items
  • Harmful chemicals
  • Any item that may cause injury, damage, or inconvenience to others
  • Any product or activity not approved by the school authorities
6. What is the payment method during the event?

Answer: All sales will be made in cash only. Students are responsible for collecting and managing their own cash payments.

7. Are phones, tablets, or other electronic devices allowed at stalls?

Answer: No. The use of mobile phones, tablets, laptops, speakers, or other electronic devices at stalls is not permitted, unless specifically approved by the school authorities.

8. Can I change my stall details after registration?

Answer: Changes will be accepted only in exceptional cases and only after approval by the organizing committee. Please register carefully and ensure all submitted information is correct.

9. What facilities will be provided for each stall?

Answer: Each registered stall will be provided with basic facilities, including:

  • Table(s)
  • Chair(s)
  • Electricity (where required and approved)
  • Designated stall space
  • A platform to showcase your creativity, products, and innovative ideas
10. Are there prizes for the best stalls?

Answer: Yes. Outstanding stalls will receive Certificates, Prizes, and Special Recognition based on creativity, presentation, innovation, teamwork, and overall performance.

11. I have another question. Whom should I contact?

Answer: For any additional queries or assistance, please contact your respective Stall Teacher In-charge. They will guide you regarding registration, rules, and event-related information.

Still have questions?

Contact your respective Stall Teacher In-charge for additional assistance.
