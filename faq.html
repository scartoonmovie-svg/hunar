Knowledge Base

Query the system. Retrieve answers.

Who can initialize a node (register a stall)?

Any student from Grade 9 to 12 can register. You can register individually or form a team of up to 4 members. All team members must be registered during the initial registration process.

What is the network connection fee (registration fee)?

Stall charge is ₹200 per stall. This fee covers space allocation and basic amenities (one 5A power point). Payment must be completed by August 4, 2026 to the teacher in-charge (Sneha Singh Ma'am).

Do I retain the data (profits from my stall)?

Yes! 100% of your profits belong to you. The school does not take any share of your earnings. After paying the ₹200 stall charge, all sales and profits from your stall are yours to keep. This is a great opportunity to learn business skills and earn money!

What protocols are restricted (what items are not allowed)?

The following items and activities are strictly prohibited:

  • 🚫 Weapons or dangerous items
  • 🚫 Hazardous chemicals or substances
  • 🚫 Adult content or inappropriate materials
  • 🚫 Open flame cooking (only induction allowed)
  • 🚫 High-power appliances like microwaves
  • 🚫 Items violating school policies

Please review the complete rules page for detailed guidelines.

How does the transaction system work (payment methods)?

Stall owners can accept payments in any convenient form:

  • 💵 Cash (most common and recommended)
  • 📱 UPI/Digital payments (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, etc.)
  • 💳 Card payments (if you have the facility)

Important: The school does not facilitate any transactions. All payments happen directly between customers and stall owners. You are responsible for managing your own sales and revenue.

What are the event timings?

Stalls must be set up and operational by 9:00 AM and must remain active until 2:00 PM on the event day. Early closing is not permitted without prior coordinator approval.

Can I change my stall category after registration?

Changes to stall categories may be possible if requested early and approved by coordinators. However, significant changes close to the event date may not be allowed. It's best to finalize your stall concept before registering.

What facilities will be provided at each stall?

Each stall will receive:

  • ⚡ One 5A power point (electricity connection)
  • 📍 Designated space allocation
  • 🏷️ Stall identification signage

You must provide: Your own table, decorations, products, trash bin, and any other equipment needed for your stall.

Is there a prize for the best stall?

Yes! Outstanding stalls will be recognized with awards and prizes. Categories may include Best Decorated Stall, Most Innovative Idea, Best Food Stall, Most Popular Stall, and more. Winners will be announced at the closing ceremony.
